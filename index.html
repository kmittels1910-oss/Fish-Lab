    LAB DEBRIEF

    Fish and fetal pig: compare the evidence

    Record answers separately for your instructor. Use at least one observation from the specimen for each answer.

    1. Trace food from mouth to intestine in both animals. Name two shared organs and explain where pyloric ceca fit into the fish digestive tract.
    2. Trace blood from the fish ventricle to its next destination. Compare that route with the pig after birth, and explain how the pig gets oxygen before birth.
    3. Explain how you distinguish a swim bladder from a urinary bladder by location and function.
    4. Choose a structure that was hidden when the body wall first opened. Explain which layer you moved and compare the structure’s location in the pig.
    5. Compare paired fins with paired limbs, and identify one fish structure that the pig lacks.
